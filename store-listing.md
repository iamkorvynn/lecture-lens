# LectureLens — Play Store listing

## Short description (≤ 80 chars)

Record lectures, read the live transcript, and revise with AI summaries.

## Long description

LectureLens turns your lectures into study material — on your phone, with
no account and no uploads.

Record the class and LectureLens saves the audio while your phone's own
speech recogniser transcribes it live, so you can read the lecture back
the moment class ends. Replay the recording, write your own notes, and
search everything later.

Add your own Gemini API key in Settings and LectureLens turns the
transcript into a short summary, key points, and flashcards you can flip
through and mark known or unknown. No key, no summary — the app never
fakes one, and your key stays on your device.

Everything is stored locally: your recordings, transcripts, notes,
summaries, and flashcards never leave your phone except for the
transcript text you choose to send to Gemini when you tap "Generate
summary".

Built for students who want to actually revise what they heard in class
— not just re-read a wall of text.

## Feature bullets

- Record lectures with live on-device transcription
- Play back recordings with seek
- Search lectures by title or transcript
- Your own editable notes per lecture
- AI summary, key points, and flashcards with your own Gemini API key
- Flashcard study mode with known/unknown tracking
- Everything stored locally on your device — no account needed
